🔒 Privacy Policy

All your data stays on your device by default. Toddler Trials Pro stores every entry — profiles, trackers, photos, notes — using your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or sent anywhere unless you explicitly turn on optional cloud backup (below). The developer has no access to your data and cannot see it, sell it, or share it.

No accounts, no tracking. There is no sign-up, no login, and no analytics or ad tracking of any kind. Using the app normally never requires an account.

Optional cloud backup. If you choose to connect Google Drive or iCloud in the Backup & Restore screen, a copy of your backup file is stored in your own personal cloud storage account — not on any server we control. You can disconnect at any time, and disconnecting does not delete anything already stored in your Google Drive or iCloud account; you manage that directly through Google or Apple. We never see, receive, or have access to this data at any point — it goes directly from your device to your own cloud account.

Deleting your data. Removing a child profile (🗑 Remove) permanently deletes all of that profile's trackers, photos, and logs from your device. Clearing your browser/app storage removes everything stored locally.

Backups. The Backup & Restore feature (💾) lets you export a JSON file of your own data for safekeeping, or optionally sync it to your own Google Drive or iCloud account. Local export files stay wherever you save them — we never see them.
