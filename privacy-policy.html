Toddler Trials Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Toddler Trials Pro ("the app," "we," "us") is a baby and toddler tracking application for parents, guardians, and caregivers. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

All your data stays on your device by default

Toddler Trials Pro stores every entry — child profiles, trackers, photos, notes — using your device's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or sent anywhere unless you explicitly turn on optional cloud backup (described below). We have no access to your data and cannot see it, sell it, or share it, because it never reaches us in the first place.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-up, no login, and no analytics or advertising tracking of any kind. Using the app normally never requires an account.

Optional cloud backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive or iCloud from the Backup & Restore screen, a copy of your backup file — which may include photos and any information you've entered about your child — is stored in your own personal cloud storage account, not on any server we control or operate. You can disconnect at any time. We never see, receive, or have access to this data at any point in that process; it travels directly from your device to your own Google or Apple account using their respective APIs, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Disconnecting cloud backup in the app does not delete anything already saved in your Google Drive or iCloud account — that's managed directly through Google's or Apple's own account tools, the same as any other file you've saved there.

Camera and photo access

If you choose to attach a photo to a tracker entry or the Photo Growth Gallery, the app requests access to your camera or photo library solely for that purpose. Photos are stored locally like any other entry, and are only included in a cloud backup if you have opted into that feature.

Deleting your data

Removing a child profile from within the app permanently deletes all of that profile's trackers, photos, and logs from your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser/app storage removes everything stored locally. If you've enabled cloud backup, deleting the backup file from your Google Drive or iCloud account (directly through Google's or Apple's own interface) removes it from there as well.

Children's privacy

Toddler Trials Pro is intended for use by adults — parents, guardians, and caregivers — to record information about a child in their care. It is not directed at, marketed to, or intended for use by children, and any information about a child is entered by the adult user, not collected directly from the child. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No location data
  • No data sold to third parties, ever

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and the in-app copy of this policy at the same time.
